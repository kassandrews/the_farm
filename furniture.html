The Farm — furniture

Every piece in src/content/furniture.ts, turned all four ways, drawn by the game's own renderer out of a real world. Each view names where its art comes from — own grid, mirrored e, or front view where a piece falls back to its front. Fixed at a summer midday, each piece in its default finish, so the picture changes when the furniture changes and at no other time.
